Writing Guide

1. The Core Data

Use this foundational structure across all resume templates.

Contact Info: [Full Name] | [Target Title] | [Phone] | [Email] | [Location] | [Links]
Education: [Degree], [Institution], [Graduation Date] (Include honors or high GPA)
Internships: Treat as "Professional Experience"
Structure: [Company Name] | [Internship Title] | [Start - End Date]
Action points: Focus on 3-4 bullet points starting with strong verbs (e.g., Developed, Optimized, Automated).

2. Template Variations

Tailor your resume content strategy based on the layout template chosen.

Type Focus & Strategy
Minimal ATS / Corporate Keyword Density & Structure
Use standard headings. Keep bullet points concise and mapped to job descriptions.
Developer Portfolio Technical Proficiency
Highlight projects (details, tech stack, links). Group skills by Language, Framework, and Tool.
Creative Designer Design & UX Process
Focus projects on the problem solved. Add Figma/Adobe Suite under skills.

3. Experience Drafting

Use these exact formula frameworks to construct outstanding impact bullet points:

[Company] | [Internship Title] | [Dates]

• [Achievement 1]: Achieved [Result] by [Action taken] using [Technology].

• [Achievement 2]: Collaborated with [Team Name] to improve [Process/Feature] by [Metric %].

• [Achievement 3]: Demonstrated proficiency in [Technical Skill] by completing [Project/Task].
